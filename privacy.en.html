Privacy Policy

한국어English

Last updated: 2026-11-13

1. Data Processed on Your Device

  • Installation identifier — a random identifier generated by the app. It may be processed with usage analytics where analytics is available and you opt in.
  • Learning progress — words, books, starred items, completions, streaks.
  • Settings — language, dark mode, font size, notification time.

We do not collect name, email, contact info, address, payment info, or location.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google Translate — online translation sends the selected word or sentence and the target language.
  • Microsoft speech service — high-quality voice sends the English text to read aloud and voice settings. Microphone recordings for shadowing are processed on the device and are not sent to a server.
  • archive.org · LibriVox — audiobook streaming.
  • jsDelivr · GitHub Pages — OTA content delivery (originals, columns).
  • Google Play Billing — handles subscription purchases and restoration. The app processes subscription status and purchase tokens. Google Play handles payment card details.
  • Sentry and PostHog — process technical error information and feature usage analytics, respectively, where available and with your opt-in.

3. Sign-Up

No sign-up is required. Originals and single-word definitions within Originals are available for free.

4. Data Storage

  • Local: device IndexedDB / localStorage (offline-capable).
  • The current app does not back up learning progress to a server. Google Play manages subscription transaction records.

5. Your Rights

  • In-app Settings: export / import / reset learning progress.
  • Anonymous ID — deleting the app removes all local data.
  • For privacy enquiries, contact ableleafstudio@gmail.com.

6. Payment & Refunds

Pro features, including Learning Books, vocabulary learning and review, quizzes, and high-quality voice, are offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription through Google Play. The price and currency are shown before purchase, and you can cancel at any time in Google Play settings. ableleafstudio does not directly collect or store payment information. Refunds follow Google Play policies.

7. Content Sources & Copyright

  • English text: Project Gutenberg (public domain).
  • Audio: LibriVox volunteer recordings (public domain).
  • Korean / multilingual translations: AI-assisted (Anthropic Claude) with editorial review.
  • Report inaccuracies: ableleafstudio@gmail.com.

8. Changes

Material changes will be announced in-app. Date shown at top.
